about me

A maker who leads with curiosity

I am Florence Katheu — a multidisciplinary creator driven by the belief that the best work begins with a single, courageous yes. Whether I'm crafting experiences, building solutions, or telling stories, I bring intention, warmth, and depth to everything I touch.

My work lives at the intersection of creativity and purpose — shaped by a deep curiosity about people, systems, and the small details that make something feel truly alive. I believe in doing less, but doing it beautifully.

Based in Nairobi. Open to the world.
